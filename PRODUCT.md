# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

MindRepo serves individual developers who need to retrieve, understand, and retain technical knowledge while working across desktop and mobile devices. The primary workflow is a quick refresh in the middle of development, followed by a deeper explanation or a future review session when needed.

## Product Purpose

MindRepo is a private structured developer knowledge and recall system for fast discovery, durable technical notes, and continuous card-level review. A concept can grow from a quick definition into Markdown sections, diagrams, references, version context, and independent study cards without becoming a public blog or generic dashboard.

## Positioning

Personal technical knowledge, organized for recall, verification, and revision rather than publication.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and developer-oriented. The interface should feel focused and trustworthy, with content and navigation taking precedence over decoration.

## Anti-references

Avoid enterprise-admin density, blog-first presentation, flashy gradients, glassmorphism, fake metrics, and overly animated UI.

## Design Principles

- Make retrieval feel immediate and quiet.
- Design mobile navigation as a first-class surface, not a collapsed desktop sidebar.
- Keep layered knowledge readable at a glance and approachable in depth.
- Use familiar, accessible controls so the interface disappears into the task.
- Support Persian RTL prose, English technical terms, and LTR code without compromise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, semantic controls, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support are baseline requirements. Direction-sensitive content and LTR code must remain legible alongside Persian RTL text.
